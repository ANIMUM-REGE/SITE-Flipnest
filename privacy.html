Flipnest Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

Flipnest is built so that your business data stays yours. This policy explains what the app does — and, mostly, what it doesn't do.

Data we collect

None. Flipnest has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of ours that receive your data. Everything you enter — inventory, photos, sales, mileage — is stored on your device and, if you enable it, in your personal iCloud (see below).

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Inventory items, photos, sales records, and mileage entries are stored locally.
  • Your iCloud (optional). If iCloud sync is enabled, your data syncs through your personal iCloud account under Apple's terms. We never see it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

Photos and camera

Camera and photo-library access is used only to attach pictures to your inventory items. Photos never leave your device (or your iCloud, if sync is on).

Exports

CSV exports are generated on your device and shared only where you send them.

Children

Flipnest is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
