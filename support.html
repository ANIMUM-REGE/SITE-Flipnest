Frequently asked

Are the platform fees exact?

FlipTally ships with each platform's published fee rates and updates them with app updates. Marketplaces change fees and some categories differ, so every rate is editable: Settings → Platform fee schedules. You can also tap the fee amount on any individual sale to correct it.

Is the Schedule C export tax advice?

No — it's a clean summary of your recorded numbers (gross receipts, cost of goods sold, fees, mileage) to hand to your tax preparer. Confirm treatment with a professional.

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new phone?

Settings → Restore purchases, signed into the same Apple Account.

Where is my data stored?

On your device (and your personal iCloud if sync is enabled). We run no servers and can't see your data — see the privacy policy.

How do I cancel?

App Store app → your profile → Subscriptions → FlipTally Pro → Cancel. You keep Pro until the period ends.
